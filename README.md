# TasteMatch

### AI-powered food discovery that understands what you're actually craving.

TasteMatch is an independent product concept exploring how AI could enhance Zomato's food discovery experience.

Instead of making users browse through hundreds of restaurants and menus, TasteMatch understands natural-language intent — including mood, cuisine, dietary preferences, budget, delivery time, familiarity, and novelty — and turns it into personalised restaurant and dish recommendations.

## What it does

- 🧠 Understands natural-language food cravings
- 🎯 Extracts user preferences and constraints
- 🍽️ Recommends restaurants and specific dishes
- 📊 Generates an explainable TasteMatch score
- 💡 Explains why each recommendation fits
- 🆕 Helps users discover restaurants they haven't tried
- ❤️ Learns from user feedback and preferences
- 🎲 Provides an intelligent "Surprise Me" experience
- 🛒 Includes a simulated ordering flow

## Product Concept

The core product loop is:

**User Intent → AI Understanding → Recommendation → Decision → Order → Feedback → Personalisation**

The goal is to explore how Zomato could evolve from search and discovery into an intelligent food decision engine.
